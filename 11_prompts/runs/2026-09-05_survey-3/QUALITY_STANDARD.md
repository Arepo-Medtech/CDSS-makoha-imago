# QUALITY_STANDARD — the Q-lines survey-3 judges against

Run: 2026-09-05_survey-3 · Phase 0 step 2 · Status: Proposed (survey instrument; not a register). Method: for each architect bullet of PROMPT-SURVEY-3 v1.0 `<quality_standard>` (as re-scoped there), one or more **Q-D-nn** (document) or **Q-F-nn** (folder) lines. Each line carries: what it tests · the layer and bullet it derives from · ≥2 in-repository evidence points that an exemplar already satisfies it (path + quoted line, observed by `sed`/`grep` this run) **or** the law that requires it **or** `[ASSESSOR-PROPOSED]` · the measurement or test (tool in `tools/`) · the threshold (`[ASSESSOR-PROPOSED]` unless a law sets it) · the classes it applies to (CLASS_CONTRACTS.md labels). Parity lines P-D-01..16 / P-F-01..10 (survey-2) are **inherited, not restated**; a Q-line may reference a P-line and raise its bar. Severity → weight mapping is v1.0's (CRITICAL ≥4 · WARNING 3 · OPTIMISATION ≤2; weight = min(5, criticality + radius)). Laws 16–17 (3.1 D-2/D-3 as corrected by 3.2) apply to every row.

Threshold policy for Layer 3 readability (stated once, rejected alternative recorded): `readability.py` computes both average sentence length (ASL) and a Flesch–Kincaid grade over prose only. The FK formula counts syllables heuristically and is inflated by identifiers (`REG-POSTURE`, `HARDEN-3.1`) that pervade this corpus — 70 of 154 scored files exceed FK 14, including every corpus volume. The **primary** threshold is therefore ASL ≤ 35 words `[ASSESSOR-PROPOSED]`; FK is reported beside it and flagged only when both exceed (FK > 20 and ASL > 35). Alternative rejected: FK ≤ 14 alone — it would file READABILITY-DENSE against 70 files, most of them the exemplars, which is noise by v1.0's own `<what_a_wrong_answer_costs>`.
