## Notes on use
- P-D-05/P-D-08/P-D-09 are the three lines that most separate 01_–03_ from 04_–10_ *by contract* rather than by size; the visible 20–60× byte gap is the symptom of those three (and of P-F-01/02/04/05 companions), not a finding in itself.
- A retained-verbatim file (MT2 directive; Arch-derived sections in 07_) is measured on its *companion set* (P-F lines) and on the honesty of its retention notice (P-D-02, P-D-13), never on its own text (law 5).
- `.mermaid`/`.html`/`.json`/`.yaml` files: P-D-01 is judged on the header comment / `$id`+`title` / top-of-file banner in lieu of YAML frontmatter (Phase 1 step 2).
