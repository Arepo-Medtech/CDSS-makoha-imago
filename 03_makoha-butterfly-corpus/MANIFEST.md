## Reading order for prompt work

1. MAK-FFC (host law) → MAK-MIF (flight doctrine) → MAK-LWC + MAK-RWC (the wings).
2. MAK-CEC (engine plane) → MAK-HDC / MAK-TXC / MAK-ABC (the faces).
3. MAK-PRB / MAK-LBP (the UIs) → MAK-LEG (the stack).
4. MAK-ANT last and always: the regulatory citation surface, precedence rules, and open assumptions that bind everything above.

## Precedence in one paragraph

MAK-FFC v1.1 is the host: no subordinate volume relaxes a corpus MUST. Consolidation volumes (CEC, HDC, TXC, ABC) never retire source requirements — their maps are cross-walks, and the host document governs any apparent difference. REG-POSTURE v1.0 (folded in MAK-ANT) governs regulatory content: assume inclusion, glass-box design target, J-1/J-2 as lower-/higher-class included, and ASSUME-REG-001..007 open until written counsel attestation. Defaults in MAK-LEG are suggestions; only its bindings are law.
