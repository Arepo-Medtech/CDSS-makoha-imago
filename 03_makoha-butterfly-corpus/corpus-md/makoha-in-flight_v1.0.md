# Mākoha in Flight

Two wings, one body. The living CDSS ecosystem is the thorax; meta-rationality is the right wing; fuzzy logic is the left. Neither wing is judged against the other — each is painted in its own colours, for what it is good for — and flight is the coordination.

> Neither wing flies. Coordination flies. The body — fabric, ledger, evaluator, knowledge plane — is what both wings are anchored to and what both wings move.

## 01 — The body, briefly

Everything both wings touch passes through one thorax: the justification fabric (every claim an argument — SPINE-1), the versioned knowledge plane (SPINE-5/6), the deterministic evaluator ("only arithmetic releases" — SPINE-7), and the append-only ledger (SPINE-4) read by three faces in three registers (SPINE-3). The body is *living* because it metabolizes: deviations feed guideline change (AF-5), corruption findings feed rebuttals (EN-5), field evidence feeds validation (GPP-13). A wing that beats without the body moves air and nothing else; every colour below is painted onto a structure the body already carries.

## 02 — The two wings, each painted for what it is good for

### The Left Wing — Fuzzy Logic
*The semantics of degree · what it is good for*

- **Gradedness — borderline becomes a place, not an error.** Partial satisfaction of a criterion is representable: μ=0.6 "elevated" is data, not noise. The cliff between 139 and 141 mmHg dissolves into a slope the system can see.
  `→ grounds annotations; warrant-applicability grading (FZ-1/3)`
- **Hedge language, computable without distortion.** Guidelines already speak fuzzily — "consider in elderly patients," "mild," "suboptimal control." Membership functions let the Guideline Compiler carry those words into GenericArguments as themselves, instead of forcing false crispness at authoring time.
  `→ EN-3; IEEE 1855 FML artifacts (FZ-2)`
- **Computing with Words — linguistic in, linguistic out.** Zadeh's programme (from manipulating measurements to manipulating perceptions) and Mendel's Perceptual Computer give the plain register a mathematics: an encoder from words, a reasoning engine over word-models, a decoder back to words a person recognizes. The patient face's "a little high today" can be a computed object with provenance, not copywriting.
  `→ SPINE-3 plain register; PF-2/5`
- **Personalized semantics — words mean different things to different people.** Personalized-individual-semantics models calibrate what *this* patient means by "severe" or "sometimes," and 2026 work grades the credibility of a membership function itself. The patient face can learn a person's vocabulary without ever inferring their values (PF-3 stays governed).
  `→ patient-face intake calibration; consensus in MDT scoring`
- **Z-numbers — the voice note that carries its own reliability.** Zadeh's Z = (restriction, reliability): "my sleep was poor *(and I'm fairly sure)*." Patient-reported and secondhand grounds arrive as value-plus-confidence pairs — a formal container for exactly the data low-resource and virtual care run on, with a decade of medical decision-making methods behind it.
  `→ grounds schema for patient-reported data; RPM annotation`
- **Type-2 bands — disagreement about meaning, held formally.** When experts disagree where "elderly" begins, an interval type-2 set stores the band instead of forcing the point. The community's spread becomes an object the knowledge plane can hold and revisit.
  `→ CF-6 disagreement; SPINE-6 pluralism (deferred trigger per MAK-DOT)`
- **Boundary cartography — a map of where the system is fragile.** Membership supports mark precisely where cliff-effect cases live. The corruption engine gets a hunting map for free.
  `→ EN-5 boundary-sweep class (FZ-5)`
- **White-box arithmetic — transparency by construction.** Zero learned parameters; every inference a readable rule; an IEEE standard (1855-2016) for serializing the whole knowledge base. Natively SPINE-7- and J-1-compatible, and auditable to the curve.
  `→ deterministic evaluator; AF-7 conformity bundles`
- **Frontier shimmer — fuzzy predicates as the LLM's leash.** 2026 neuro-symbolic work has LLMs extract fuzzy symptom patterns from clinical narrative and decode them into a symbolic fuzzy-logic knowledge base whose inference paths are auditable and physician-correctable — the newest paint on this wing, and it beats in the corpus's own rhythm: the LLM proposes, the symbolic layer verifies.
  `→ EN-6 authoring/extraction; SPINE-7 preserved`

### The Right Wing — Meta-Rationality
*The judgment of systems · what it is good for (as folded into MAK-FFC)*

- **System choice — knowing what the situation needs.** The defining meta-rational act: judging which rational system applies, when, and how far. The corpus holds plural guideline lineages side by side and refuses to auto-resolve their conflicts — choice stays a visible, human, recorded act.
  `→ SPINE-6; XC-4 pluralist localization`
- **Justified deviation — discretion given a data structure.** GAAM's generic/actual split turns wise departure from a guideline into a first-class, warrant-level, reviewable object instead of an exception outside the system. The system computerizes criteria, never discretion — and gives discretion somewhere to live.
  `→ SPINE-8; Deviation Composer (CF-3); AF-3 reframing`
- **Ontological remodeling, governed.** Categories are human artifacts that wear out (the ICSD-2 freeze). The corpus makes remodeling a ritual with evidence: deviation aggregates → change proposal → ratification → new version, old versions pinned forever.
  `→ AF-5; SPINE-5 replayability`
- **Boundary work — respect for the circumrational.** The interface where the nebulous patient meets the formal system is where CDSS live or die. Intake at home, assimilable briefs, read-and-decide consultations, no naked scores — the Blake lessons, made normative.
  `→ CF-1/2; PF-1/5`
- **Trading zones — one argument, many tongues.** Patient, clinician, and auditor speak different registers of the same argument object; renderers may translate but never reweight. The pidgin between expertise domains is engineered, not hoped for.
  `→ SPINE-3; anti-three-truths (SPINE-9)`
- **Reasoning communities — group judgment without false unanimity.** MDT decisions are multi-author arguments; who contributed which warrant is preserved, and disagreement is recorded rather than averaged (the Delphi lesson: consensus is not accuracy).
  `→ CF-6; Stranieri's reasoning-communities line`
- **Purposes and values as first-class.** The best clinical outcome is not always the best life outcome. Patient-authored priorities enter the system only through ratified, revocable mappings — meaning-change treated with the gravity it deserves.
  `→ PF-3; values module`
- **Anomaly vigilance — watching rationality for its failures.** The standing adversary that hunts the engine's failure regions, and the theater detector that hunts the ledger's boilerplate: meta-rational suspicion of one's own formal system, institutionalized and continuous.
  `→ EN-5 corruption engine; AF-4`

## 03 — Flight: the coordination of the wings

Eight wing-beats. In each, the left wing contributes a semantics of degree, the right wing contributes a judgment of systems, and flight is what neither could do alone.

### Beat 1 · The borderline patient
- **Left:** Grades the fit: this case satisfies the pathway's criteria to degree 0.6 — the edge is measured, not hidden.
- **Right:** Governs the crossing: partial fit routes to judgment and the Deviation Composer, never to silent forcing into a bin.
- **Flight:** The patients guidelines were never written for — the multimorbid, the atypical, the in-between — become the system's best-served cases instead of its casualties.

### Beat 2 · The full translation loop
- **Left:** Fuzzification encodes the world's vagueness into the system; Computing-with-Words decodes the system back into words a person owns.
- **Right:** Audits what every translation loses — formalization-awareness as a standing duty, with the argument recording what the encoding could not carry.
- **Flight:** The circumrational boundary — where Bayor's 37-of-40 systems die — becomes a designed, instrumented, two-way crossing.

### Beat 3 · Meaning under governance
- **Left:** Measures meaning drift: membership-credibility scoring and personalized-semantics learning detect when a curve no longer matches how the community uses the word.
- **Right:** Decides meaning change: AF-5 ratifies new curves as versioned FML artifacts; no dial, no silent tuning; old decisions replay against old meanings.
- **Flight:** The ecosystem's vocabulary stays alive without ever becoming unaccountable — living language, audited.

### Beat 4 · Conflict with a metric
- **Left:** When two guidelines both claim a patient, graded applicability shows *how much* each claims — the conflict acquires a shape.
- **Right:** SPINE-6 surfaces the conflict unresolved; a clinician's choice between systems is recorded as the meta-rational act it is.
- **Flight:** Guideline conflict stops being an alert-storm embarrassment and becomes navigable terrain with a map.

### Beat 5 · Disagreement held, not averaged
- **Left:** Type-2 bands store what the community actually has — a spread of meanings — when a single curve cannot be agreed.
- **Right:** Reasoning-community ratification adopts the band as the honest artifact; MDT records keep each author's warrant distinct.
- **Flight:** The system can be truthful about expert disagreement all the way down to the mathematics of a single word.

### Beat 6 · Reliability-aware listening
- **Left:** Z-numbers carry the patient's voice with its own stated reliability — "poor sleep, fairly sure" arrives as (restriction, confidence), not as a corrupted number.
- **Right:** The qualifier keeps belief semantics separate and honest; nothing patient-reported is flattened into false precision or discarded as soft.
- **Flight:** The patient face — and every low-resource deployment running on self-report and cheap sensors — gets a data model equal to how people actually speak.

### Beat 7 · The adversary's map
- **Left:** Membership supports chart exactly where the boundaries — and therefore the fragilities — lie.
- **Right:** The corruption engine hunts those coordinates and publishes what it kills as rebuttals every face can see.
- **Flight:** Meta-rational vigilance stops being a temperament and becomes a scheduled, targeted, evidence-producing process.

### Beat 8 · The LLM on a leash
- **Left:** 2026 neuro-symbolic frameworks extract fuzzy symptom predicates from free-text narrative and hold them in a symbolic, auditable, physician-correctable knowledge base.
- **Right:** SPINE-7 keeps the release key with the deterministic evaluator; the LLM proposes, the argument tree decides, the correction loop is governed.
- **Flight:** The frontier's most seductive capability — reading the patient's story — enters the ecosystem without ever holding the pen that signs.

## Why coordination, in one breath

> A left wing beating alone is precision about vague things inside an ontology no one is watching — curves tuned forever toward the wrong question. A right wing beating alone is wise judgment holding cliff-edged instruments — knowing the guideline doesn't quite fit, with no way to say *how much*. Together: the left wing senses in degrees, the right wing judges in systems, and the body records every beat as an argument. That is the flight — a CDSS that can be gentle at the edges, honest about disagreement, multilingual across its three faces, and accountable to the curve and the clause alike.
